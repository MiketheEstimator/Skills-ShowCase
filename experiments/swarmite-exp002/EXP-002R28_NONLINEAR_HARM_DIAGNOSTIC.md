# EXP-002R28 — Nonlinear Amplitude Harm Diagnostic

## Status
COMPLETE_NO_SIMPLE_GATE.

Using the R27 confirmation worlds only as retrospective diagnostics, harm was not explained by simple nonlinear effect composition. Correlation of treatment-minus-control edge error with true effect counts was approximately: linear -0.088, tanh +0.285, quadratic +0.019, total edge count +0.209. Harm worlds averaged 1.83 tanh edges versus 0.72 in non-harm worlds, but the pattern was not sufficient: one large-harm world was purely linear and the catastrophic seed 66012 had only one tanh edge.

A controller-available observational residual-nonlinearity score was also not useful. Its correlation with edge-error delta was -0.340 and mean score was lower in harm worlds (0.379) than non-harm worlds (0.450), opposite the proposed gate direction.

Conclusion: do not fit an amplitude gate to this small diagnostic. The 2x amplitude policy remains strongly supported on correctly specified linear benchmark-v2, but nonlinear transfer is unresolved and heterogeneous. Redirect to a distinct transfer axis.
